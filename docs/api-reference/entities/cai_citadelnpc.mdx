### CAI_CitadelNPC.SetSummoner() {#setsummoner}

Sets who the NPC works for, usually a hero pawn, or clears it with null. With `SetMinion`, the hero gets credit for its damage and kills. Minions are removed with the pawn, such as on a hero swap, but survive the hero's death.
