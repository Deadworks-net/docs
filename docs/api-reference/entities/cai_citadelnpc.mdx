## Methods

### CAI_CitadelNPC.SetMinion() {#setminion}

Makes the NPC a minion, like Necro's skeletons, or back into a regular NPC. A minion's damage and kills are credited to its `Summoner` while the summoner is alive, it also attacks neutral creeps, and enemies' abilities treat it as a minion rather than a trooper.

### CAI_CitadelNPC.SetSummoner() {#setsummoner}

Sets who the NPC works for, usually a hero pawn. Together with `SetMinion`, that hero gets the credit for its damage and kills. When the pawn entity is removed (for example on a hero swap) the game removes its minions too; they stay alive through the hero's death. Null clears it.
